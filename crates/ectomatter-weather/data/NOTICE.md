# meteoalarm-regions.json

The warning regions (EMMA_ID areas) MeteoAlarm's country feeds name, with their
outlines simplified to about 500 meters and rounded to three decimals, so a
place can be matched to the region its warnings are issued for.

Derived from `assets/geocodes.json` in the `meteoalarm` Python package 0.3.0
(https://github.com/NiklasJordan/meteoalarm), MIT License,
